Design initiative · contact step

AutoVex exclusive

Would sellers agree to sell their car only on AutoVex in exchange for better visibility among buyers? A painted-door test on the contact step: we offer the deal, count who takes it, and build nothing behind it yet.

Status: painted-door test · 3 variants · copy is a draft October 2026

What this test is

It measures interest, nothing else. Every seller on the contact step sees an offer: sell only on AutoVex, and your listing gets better visibility among buyers. The share who tick it tells us whether exclusivity is worth building.

Nothing sits behind the door in this test. Ticking the box does not confirm exclusivity, nothing checks it, and the extra visibility is not built on the buyer side. The selection is not shown anywhere else in the product.

The failure mode is an offer nobody sees. An unseen offer would read as no interest, and the test would answer the wrong question.

Two variants, two kinds of signal. Variant 1 is an optional checkbox: a tick is a clear yes, but an empty box can mean no, didn't notice, or didn't care. Variant 2 asks it as a required yes-or-no question, so every seller who submits has seen it and answered it. Both variants limit exclusivity to the auction, with its typical length, so it never reads as a permanent commitment.

1 — Variant 1: optional exclusivity checkbox

Draft · draft copyContact stepEvery seller, new ad or edit

What changes

  • A new block between the email field and the consent checkboxes: light-blue fill, blue border, and a green “recommended” tag on its top-right corner, overlapping the edge so it reads as added to the block rather than part of its content.
  • The whole block is one checkbox. Its label is the headline, and it names the condition and the reward together. The description sits under it. Clicking anywhere on the block ticks it.
  • The checkbox is 20px with a field-weight border, larger than the consent ticks below it, so it reads as a checkbox at the headline's size. Hover and ticked: blue border, as every selected option in the funnel.
  • Unchecked by default. Not required: submitting without it works exactly as today.
  • Shown to every seller, logged in or not. A logged-in seller has no consent checkboxes, but still gets this block.
  • Editable. A seller who comes back to the contact step sees their earlier choice and can change it.
  • Shown nowhere else. No badge on the car card, no change to the success page, offers page or emails.

Acceptance criteria

  • Control sellers see the contact step exactly as today.
  • Variant sellers see the block above the consent checkboxes, logged in or not, unchecked on first visit.
  • The checkbox is optional; validation and the submit button are unchanged.
  • Returning to the contact step shows the seller's last choice, and it can be changed.
  • The choice is recorded per ad with the arm, so the opt-in rate can be read per arm.
  • Nothing else in the product changes based on the choice.

Why: exclusivity changes what AutoVex can promise buyers and sellers both; this tells us whether sellers would take the trade before anything is built.

2 — Variant 2: required visibility choice

Draft · draft copyContact stepEvery seller, new ad or edit

What changes

  • A required yes-or-no question in the same place, between the email field and the consent checkboxes, with the funnel's group-legend heading and a red asterisk: “Haluatko autollesi lisänäkyvyyttä?” (do you want extra visibility for your car?).
  • Two option cards, the funnel's own radio cards: yes, on the condition first with the green “recommended” tag, no second. Each has a short description, and no explanation sits below them. Neither is preselected.
  • Both answers are in the seller's own voice. The yes option names the condition, “Kyllä, myyn vain AutoVexissä” (yes, I'll sell only on AutoVex), since the question already names the reward; the no option is simply “En halua lisänäkyvyyttä” (I don't want extra visibility). Both descriptions are first person too.
  • The time limit is in the yes description: “tarjouskilpailun ajan (noin 36 tuntia)”, during the auction. It starts with the auction, so a seller with ads elsewhere is not asked to take them down before choosing.
  • The standard option does not mention selling elsewhere, so it cannot read as advice to list the car on other sites.
  • Submitting without a choice is blocked with the funnel's own “selection required” error on the options.
  • Shown to every seller, logged in or not, and the earlier choice is restored when they come back to edit.
  • Shown nowhere else, as in variant 1.

3 — Variant 3: optional section, wider commitment until the offers expire

Draft · draft copyContact stepEvery seller, new ad or edit

What changes, compared with variant 1

  • Same blue block, standard checkbox. The block keeps variant 1's light-blue fill and blue border, but the checkbox and its label are the contact step's own consent checkbox (16px box, 16px medium label), with the description in 14px under it. The section heading above carries the value, so the tick does not need to.
  • No tag. A section heading above the block instead, the same shape as the photos step's optional section (“Valinnaiset lisäkuvat”): a 20px bold heading, “Valinnainen lisänäkyvyys” (optional extra visibility), and one line on the benefit. It separates the optional offer from the required contact fields above it, and names it as optional rather than recommending it.
  • Each line says one thing. The heading names the option, its line gives the benefit, the checkbox label is the condition alone (“Myyn vain AutoVexissä”, I'll sell only on AutoVex), and the description gives the commitment's scope and period. The section above carries the value, so the label does not repeat it.
  • A wider commitment. The seller neither lists the car for sale nor sells it elsewhere, where variant 1 names selling only.
  • A longer, more honest period. The commitment runs through the auction and while the offers are valid, about 5 business days, where variant 1 says the auction only, about 36 hours.

Checkbox label (the headline)

Myyn vain AutoVexissä

I'll sell only on AutoVex

Description

En ilmoita autoani myyntiin enkä myy sitä muualla tarjouskilpailun ja tarjousten voimassaolon aikana (noin 5 arkipäivää).

I won't list or sell my car elsewhere during the auction and while the offers are valid (about 5 business days).

Where “about 5 business days” comes from: an auction is scheduled to end three to four business days after it starts, sooner once the first offer arrives, and the seller then has 24 hours to decide. Five business days is the honest upper end, which is the right figure for a commitment. A final offer or a negotiation can extend it.

Acceptance criteria

  • As variant 1, with the section heading, label and description above and no tag.
  • The choice is recorded with the arm, so variant 1 and variant 3 opt-in rates can be compared.

Why: the real feature would need sellers not to list the car elsewhere, for as long as the offers stand; this tests interest in that ask rather than in a lighter one.

What to read from it

  • Opt-in rate: in variants 1 and 3, ticked out of sellers who submitted the step; in variant 2, the exclusive answer out of all answers. Variant 1 against variant 3 shows what the wider, longer commitment costs in opt-ins.
  • Did the block cost anything? Contact-step completion, variant against control. A commitment offer next to the submit button could make some sellers hesitate.
  • Who opts in: split by review segment and by the seller-intent answer where one exists, to see whether committed sellers are also the ones ready to sell.

Open questions

  • The promise is not kept in this test. Sellers who tick it are told their listing gets more prominence, and it does not. Do we tell them afterwards, for example on the success page or in a later email, or leave it as is for the test's length?
  • Variants 1 and 2 say about 36 hours, variant 3 about 5 business days. They describe different periods (the auction only, against the auction plus the offers' validity), so both are correct for their wording. The funnel and emails elsewhere tell sellers the auction typically lasts 36 hours.
  • “Noin 36 tuntia” is what sellers are told today, not what the auction does. The same figure appears in the funnel and emails, but the auction is scheduled to end three to four business days after it starts, and the first offer brings that forward to one or two business days. Worth checking against real auction durations before the test runs.
  • Option order in variant 2. The exclusive option is first, and the first option tends to be picked more. Randomising the order per seller would remove that bias from the result.
  • Was variant 1 seen? Its opt-in rate only means something among sellers who scrolled to it. Recording when it comes into view would separate “not interested” from “never saw it”. Variant 2 does not need this.
  • One variant or two? A quieter version (option card only, no block) would show how much of the opt-in rate is the presentation.
