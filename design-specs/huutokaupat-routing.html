Design initiative · front page

Huutokaupat routing

A seller whose car has been driven 280 000 km or more is stopped by an error on the front page form today, with nowhere to go. This initiative removes that error and sends them to Huutokaupat.com, a separate service that can sell their car. The team has chosen the design below; it ships as an A/B test.

Status: decided · Variant 6 · ready to build as an A/B test October 2026

How to read this

Two changes, both on the front page form. Change 1 takes away the mileage error and fixes where the limit falls; change 2 is the screen the seller lands on instead.

In the prototype both live behind Huutokaupat routing → Variant 6 on the prototype bar. Earlier alternatives were reviewed by the team and set aside; this page describes only the one going ahead. With no variant selected the front page behaves as production does today, including the bug described in change 1. Finnish copy carries a grey English gloss for reading only; the gloss is not copy.

What we are solving

  • A dead end on the first screen. A high-mileage seller types their mileage, sees a red “we can't sell this” line under the field and has nothing to press. The error tells them no, but not what to do instead.
  • Give them a real next step. Huutokaupat.com runs auctions for private sellers and takes high-mileage cars. Pointing the seller there turns a rejection into a recommendation.
  • Be clear that it is someone else. Huutokaupat.com is a separate company. The screen says so, so the seller does not expect AutoVex to handle the sale from there.

How it ships

  • As a VWO A/B test, most likely split 99% to the variant and 1% to control, if VWO allows it. VWO does not allow 100% to one arm, so a small control arm is how the variant reaches almost every seller.
  • The variant is changes 1 and 2 below.
  • Control is the front page form exactly as production has it today, including the 280 000 km bug in change 1. Nothing in control changes.

1 - High mileage is no longer a form error

Where: front page form · mileage field Scenario: mileage 280 000 km or more

A bug in production today, at exactly 280 000 km

  • The form shows the “only cars under 280 000 km” error as soon as the mileage reaches 280 000.
  • But the form still submits at exactly 280 000: the listing is created and the seller is taken into the funnel, with the error still on screen as they leave.
  • Only from 280 001 km is the seller actually stopped, because the server accepts 280 000 and refuses anything above it.

So today the error and the outcome disagree for one value. The variant removes that disagreement: 280 000 km and above go to the Huutokaupat.com screen, and no error is shown on the form.

What changes

  • The mileage field accepts any number. No error and no red outline appear while typing, however high the value.
  • The form submits. Where it goes depends on the mileage: 280 000 km or more opens the screen in change 2 and creates no listing; anything lower continues to the funnel exactly as today.
  • Everything else on the form stays: the registration number is still checked first, and an empty mileage still shows its own error.

After

No message. The line is removed from this form in the variant.

Try it in the prototype - enter any valid registration number (e.g. ABC-123) and a mileage of 280 000 or more. Control reproduces today’s bug: 280 000 shows the error and still continues to the funnel, 280 001 is stopped.

Acceptance criteria

  • Typing 280 000 or more into the mileage field shows no error and no red outline.
  • With a valid registration number, submitting at exactly 280 000 km opens the change 2 screen and does not create a listing.
  • Submitting above 280 000 km does the same.
  • At 279 999 km and below the seller continues to the funnel as today.
  • An invalid registration number and an empty mileage show their errors as today, and the form does not submit.
  • The limit stays the country setting it is today (280 000 km in Finland), not a number written into the form.
  • Control is unchanged, bug included.

Why: An error is the right tool for a typo, not for a car that is simply outside what we sell. Blocking the form leaves the seller with nothing to do. And the copy says “under 280 000 km”, so 280 000 itself belongs on the Huutokaupat.com side.

2 - Huutokaupat.com screen

Where: front page, after the form is submitted Scenario: valid registration number, mileage 280 000 km or more

What changes

  • A full-screen white view replaces the page, in the same shell as production’s “Haetaan autosi perustiedot” (Fetching your car's basic details) loading screen: AutoVex logo at the top, the content in one left-aligned column, centred on the screen. The same layout at every width.
  • First, the offers page’s red draft notification, the one a seller sees there when their ad is rejected: 2px red border, a bold title and grey text, without its own button. Top left, the registration number as typed. Top right, the mileage as typed in a red tag with a prohibit icon, so a seller who mistyped (800 000 for 80 000) sees the figure that stopped them. Then “Pahoittelut!” and the limit sentence.
  • The title “Suosittelemme Huutokaupat.comia” and a three-item checklist of what Huutokaupat.com offers. Plain grey checks, not AutoVex blue, and the second item names Huutokaupat.com, so the list cannot be read as AutoVex’s own offer.
  • Huutokaupat.com’s own button: full width, 56px tall, in their dark green (#142C10) with their white logo and an arrow, so it reads as leaving AutoVex. It opens Huutokaupat.com in the same tab. No automatic redirect: the seller always chooses.
  • “Takaisin etusivulle” (Back to the front page), centred under the button, closes the screen. The logo does the same.
  • A small disclaimer at the bottom says the two services are separate.
  • Nothing is saved: no listing is started, and the registration number and mileage are not passed on.

Acceptance criteria

  • The screen covers the whole viewport on a white background, logo at the top, content centred, at every width from 360px up.
  • The notification shows the registration number and the mileage exactly as the seller typed them, the mileage with thousands separated and “km” after it, never wrapping across lines.
  • The button opens https://kamppis.huutokaupat.com/yksityishenkilon-autohuutokauppa in the same tab. Nothing opens automatically.
  • “Takaisin etusivulle” and the logo close the screen and leave the seller on the front page with the form as they filled it, focus in the mileage field.
  • Coming back from Huutokaupat.com with the browser's back button shows the front page, not this screen.
  • Screen readers meet it as a dialog with the title focused when it opens.
  • No listing is created and nothing about the car is stored or sent.

Why: The red notification is a pattern sellers already meet on the offers page, so the stop reads as AutoVex’s own and is softened by an apology rather than a bare error. Marking the typed mileage covers the seller who simply mistyped: they see the figure, go back, and the mileage field is where they land. The button in Huutokaupat.com’s own colours makes it clear the seller is leaving us.

What stays the same

  • Below 280 000 km nothing changes. The seller sees production's “Haetaan autosi perustiedot” (Fetching your car's basic details) screen while the car's details load, then the equipment step. The prototype now shows that screen too, for a fixed 5 seconds; production keeps it up until the car lookup returns, which can take up to around 25 seconds and is near-instant when the car's details are already known.
  • The registration number is checked before the mileage, as today.

Notes for whoever builds this

  • Exactly 280 000 km goes to Huutokaupat.com. Production disagrees with itself here today (see change 1): the front page form and the funnel's own first step show the error at 280 000, while the server and the price estimate page accept it. The variant settles it on the copy's side: “alle 280 000 km” (under 280 000 km), so 280 000 is routed.
  • Only the front page form is in scope. The same limit and the same error also appear on the funnel's first step (reached when the funnel opens without a registration number and mileage) and on the price estimate page. See the open questions.
  • The server check stays. A listing over the limit must still be refused when saved; this change only decides what the seller sees before that.

Open questions

  • How does Huutokaupat.com know the visit came from us? The link has no tracking parameter yet. Whatever they need, a campaign parameter or a referral code, has to be agreed and added to the link.
  • Do we measure it ourselves? An analytics event when the screen opens, when the seller presses the button, and when they go back, would show how many leave and how many return.
  • Should the registration number and mileage go with the seller? It would save them typing it again. It also means sharing data with another company, so it needs a privacy decision first.
  • Should the funnel's first step and the price estimate page route the same way? Both stop high mileage with the same error today.
  • Naming another company in the product needs legal and brand sign-off before production.
