Design initiative · front page

Huutokaupat routing

A seller whose car has been driven 280 000 km or more is stopped by an error on the front page form today, with nowhere to go. This initiative removes that error and sends them to Huutokaupat.com, a separate service that can sell their car.

Status: in the prototype · copy pending approval October 2026

How to read this

Two changes, both on the front page form. Change 1 takes away the mileage error; change 2 is the screen the seller lands on instead.

In the prototype both live behind a variant: Huutokaupat routing → Variant 1 on the prototype bar. Variant 2 is the same with one difference in how the typed mileage is shown, and Variant 3 is a more illustrated layout with no automatic redirect; see the alternatives under change 2. With no variant selected the front page behaves as production does today. Finnish copy carries a grey English gloss for reading only; the gloss is not copy.

What we are solving

  • A dead end on the first screen. A high-mileage seller types their mileage, sees a red “we can't sell this” line under the field and has nothing to press. The error tells them no, but not what to do instead.
  • Give them a real next step. Huutokaupat.com runs auctions for private sellers and takes high-mileage cars. Pointing the seller there turns a rejection into a recommendation.
  • Be clear that it is someone else. Huutokaupat.com is a separate company. The screen says so, so the seller does not expect AutoVex to handle the sale from there.

1 - High mileage is no longer a form error

Where: front page form · mileage field Scenario: mileage 280 000 km or more

What changes

  • The mileage field accepts any number. No error appears while typing, however high the value.
  • The form submits. Where it goes depends on the mileage: 280 000 km or more opens the screen in change 2; anything lower continues to the funnel exactly as today.
  • Everything else on the form stays: the registration number is still checked first, and an empty mileage still shows its own error.

Today · under the mileage field, shown while typing

Valitettavasti kauttamme voi myydä vain alle 280 000 km ajettuja autoja

Unfortunately only cars driven under 280 000 km can be sold through our service

After

No message. The line is removed from this form.

Try it in the prototype - enter any valid registration number (e.g. ABC-123) and a mileage of 280 000 or more.

Acceptance criteria

  • Typing 280 000 or more into the mileage field shows no error and no red outline.
  • With a valid registration number, submitting at 280 000 km or more opens the change 2 screen; below 280 000 km the seller continues to the funnel as today.
  • An invalid registration number and an empty mileage show their errors as today, and the form does not submit.
  • The limit stays the country setting it is today (280 000 km in Finland), not a number written into the form.

Why: An error is the right tool for a typo, not for a car that is simply outside what we sell. Blocking the form leaves the seller with nothing to do.

2 - Huutokaupat.com screen with automatic redirect

Where: front page, after the form is submitted Scenario: valid registration number, mileage 280 000 km or more Copy pending approval

What changes

  • A full-screen white view replaces the page, built like production's “Haetaan autosi perustiedot” (Fetching your car's basic details) loading screen: AutoVex logo at the top, everything else centred horizontally and vertically. No animation.
  • The text repeats the mileage the seller typed next to the limit, so a typing mistake (800 000 for 80 000) is easy to spot before the redirect.
  • Below the title and the text, a large primary button goes to Huutokaupat.com. The button counts down from 20 seconds in its own label; at zero the seller is taken there automatically, in the same tab.
  • Directly under it, a text button “Takaisin etusivulle” (Back to the front page) cancels the countdown and returns to the front page. The logo does the same.
  • A small disclaimer at the bottom says the two services are separate.
  • Nothing is saved: no listing is started, and the registration number and mileage are not passed on.

Acceptance criteria

  • The screen covers the whole viewport on a white background, with the logo at the top and the content centred both ways, at every width from 360px up.
  • The button label shows the seconds left, starting at 20 and updating every second. At zero the same tab opens https://kamppis.huutokaupat.com/yksityishenkilon-autohuutokauppa.
  • The text shows the mileage exactly as the seller typed it, with thousands separated and “km” after it, never wrapping across lines.
  • Pressing the button opens the same page at once, in the same tab.
  • “Takaisin etusivulle” and the logo stop the countdown, close the screen and leave the seller on the front page with the form as they filled it, focus in the mileage field.
  • Coming back from Huutokaupat.com with the browser's back button shows the front page, not this screen stuck at zero.
  • Screen readers meet it as a dialog with the title focused when it opens; the countdown stays visible in the button the whole time.
  • No listing is created and nothing about the car is stored or sent.

Why: The screen follows the loading screen's layout so it reads as AutoVex's own next step rather than an ad. The countdown in the button means the redirect is never a surprise, and the back button is right under it so it is easy to say no. Showing the typed mileage covers the seller who simply mistyped: they see the figure, go back, and the mileage field is where they land.

What stays the same

  • Below 280 000 km nothing changes. The seller sees production's “Haetaan autosi perustiedot” (Fetching your car's basic details) screen while the car's details load, then the equipment step. The prototype now shows that screen too, for a fixed 5 seconds; production keeps it up until the car lookup returns, which can take up to around 25 seconds and is near-instant when the car's details are already known.
  • The registration number is checked before the mileage, as today.

Notes for whoever builds this

  • Production disagrees with itself about exactly 280 000 km. The front page form and the funnel's own first step reject 280 000, while the server and the price estimate page accept it. This change follows the front page: 280 000 and above go to Huutokaupat.com. If the limit is meant to include 280 000, the copy's “alle 280 000 km” (under 280 000 km) needs to change with it.
  • Only the front page form is in scope. The same limit and the same error also appear on the funnel's first step (reached when the funnel opens without a registration number and mileage) and on the price estimate page. See the open questions.
  • The server check stays. A listing over the limit must still be refused when saved; this change only decides what the seller sees before that.

Open questions

  • How does Huutokaupat.com know the visit came from us? The link has no tracking parameter yet. Whatever they need, a campaign parameter or a referral code, has to be agreed and added to the link.
  • Do we measure it ourselves? An analytics event when the screen opens, when the seller presses the button, when the countdown sends them, and when they go back, would show how many leave and how many return.
  • Should the registration number and mileage go with the seller? It would save them typing it again. It also means sharing data with another company, so it needs a privacy decision first.
  • Should the funnel's first step and the price estimate page route the same way? Both stop high mileage with the same error today.
  • Naming another company in the product needs legal and brand sign-off before production.
