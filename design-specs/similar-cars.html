Design initiative · proposal for review

Similar cars

Sellers wonder what price to expect. We show them the highest offers dealerships made on AutoVex for cars like theirs: as a table on the offers page and the decision page, and as one average figure while their listing is being received.

The proposal

Three places, one block of data. Everything in this section and the next four is what we propose to build. Anything else lives at the bottom of the page, under Other considerations.

A table, 3 cars

The 3 newest similar cars for each of the seller's cars in review or at auction, with each car's highest offer. No "Näytä aiemmat".

3 · Submit loader

Design

True everywhere

  • The figure is each car's highest offer, labelled with production's own "Korkein tarjous". Never an accepted price or a sale price.
  • Latest first, in three recency bands: "Viimeisen 7 päivän aikana", "Viimeisen kuukauden aikana", "Yli kuukausi sitten", counted in days back from today.
  • Hidden below 3 similar cars. One or two cars read as a quote for the seller's own car, not as evidence.
  • Only while the seller is forming an expectation: ad in review, auction live, offers waiting, negotiating. Never once they have accepted or rejected.
  • What counts as "similar" is Analytics' question. The design works whether the answer is "same model only" or "comparable models".

Not in this proposal: the photo-card version of the list, showing the accepted offer, and the other submit-loader shapes. They are parked at the bottom of the page.

Open questions for this proposal

Each one has to be answered before the build, or decided as part of it.

For Analytics

  • How similar? Same model and specification, same model only, or comparable models, and which year and mileage band around the seller's car.
  • How many cars, over what period? This decides whether 3 is the right minimum and whether 20 is reachable for a typical car.
  • Average or median on the submit loader? "Keskimäärin" describes an average (mean). A median, which one very high offer moves less, would need other words.
  • Spread. If a list ranges widely, it may anchor the seller to the top rather than inform. Should cars outside a band be left out?

For the team

  • The loader delays email verification. A seller who still has to verify their address reaches the success page, and the verification card, 11.5 seconds later.
  • The loader moves on by itself. A normal loader pattern once the work is done, but a seller reading the card is moved mid-sentence. 10 seconds is meant to be enough.
  • The loader's figure says "tarjottu" while it is the average of the highest offers only. A seller may read it as all offers.
  • With fewer than 3 similar cars, the loader still runs, without the card. Should it be skipped instead?
  • How many does each "Näytä aiemmat" add on the decision page? The prototype adds 5.
  • Copy marked draft in the copy table needs approval.

1 — Offers page: the table

Its own section, "Vastaavien autojen tarjouksia", directly under the seller's own cars and above the FAQ.

What it shows

  • Each list opens with the seller's car: make and model in bold, then the model-year range of the list, e.g. "Volkswagen Amarok · vuosimallit 2018–2020". No intro sentence: the section heading already says these are similar cars.
  • The 3 newest cars, and no "Näytä aiemmat". The longer list is the decision page's.
  • Columns: specification · model year · mileage · highest offer. The offer is right-aligned and bold.
  • Make and model are not repeated in the rows when every car is the same model. When models differ, the first column becomes "Auto" and each row leads with make and model in bold.
  • Recency bands: each band opens with its label, left-aligned. Rows carry no rules of their own; a thin grey rule sits only above each band label, the first one being the column headings' own. A band with no cars is left out.
  • Band label: calendar icon and text, 12px medium, dark grey (slate-600), the same for every band. No fill, border, underline or blue, so it never reads as a link or a button.
  • On a phone (block narrower than 520px) year and mileage fold into a grey line under the specification, leaving two columns. No sideways scrolling.
  • A seller with several cars gets one list per car, in the order the page lists the cars (drafts in review first, then published cars). Two cars of the same make and model share one list, under the first. No registration plate on the list: it belongs to the seller's own car, and next to "similar cars" it would read as if the list were about that exact car.

3 — Submit loader: the average offer

A short screen between the contact step and the success page. It confirms the listing is being received and shows one figure: what similar cars have been offered on AutoVex on average. A teaser of data the seller will have on the offers page, which may also make finishing worth it.

Contact step · "Lähetä ilmoitus" → Submit loader · 10 s + 1.5 s → Success page, in place of the loader
The success page opens by itself, in place of this screen, so the browser's back button from the success page never returns here.

3 · On to the success page. No button.

What it shows

  • From the top: the logo, a status icon in its own row, the heading, the loading line, and the card. The heading takes the success page's own heading style (Barlow bold, 24px), so the next screen continues it.
  • While receiving: production's loading spinner (48px), "Ilmoitusta vastaanotetaan" and "Muutama sekunti jäljellä".
  • The card: the chart icon and "Vastaavista autoista on tarjottu AutoVexissä keskimäärin", then one figure in bold. The sentence says the figure is an average over other cars, so it does not read as an offer or a valuation for the seller's own car. Nothing else on the card: no range, no time period, no note.
  • The figure is the average of the highest offers of the cars the decision page would list for this car (the newest 20 at most), rounded to the nearest 100 €. With fewer than 3 similar cars, the card is left out.
  • After 10 seconds: the spinner turns into a green check-circle (56px) that draws itself, the heading becomes "Ilmoitus vastaanotettu" and the loading line goes, its space kept so the card does not move. 1.5 seconds later the success page opens by itself.
  • The AutoVex logo links to the front page. Like the move to the success page, it replaces this screen, so back from the front page goes to the contact step, never into the loader.

Submit loader rules

  • Only when the ad was really submitted. If publishing is refused for missing photos, the seller goes straight to the success page's refusal. An edit saved from the offers page returns there as before.
  • It replaces itself in the browser history, both when it moves on and when the logo is pressed, so the back button never lands on it.
  • Screen readers hear both states through a status message ("Ilmoitusta vastaanotetaan. Muutama sekunti jäljellä", then "Ilmoitus vastaanotettu"). Focus is not moved.
  • With reduced motion the spinner and the check do not animate; the timing stays.

Built from what exists

  • Table frame: the offers page's own white auction card, no border; row and band rules from the one existing consumer-side table.
  • "Näytä aiemmat": the standard secondary button.
  • Recency band label: caption text with production's calendar icon.
  • Submit loader: production's full-screen loading screen (the one after the front page's registration and mileage form), its spinner, the success page's heading style, and the check-circle icon in the success green.
  • "Mallitarkennus", "Vuosimalli", "Ajokilometrit", "Korkein tarjous" and "Muutama sekunti jäljellä" are production copy. The rest is draft.

Other considerations

Kept as a record and for later iterations. Nothing here is to be built now. Each item opens on its own; the prototype keeps them switchable from the bar's Variants menu.

Photo cards instead of the tableVariant 2 · parked, a future alternative
  • One card per car in a sideways-scrolling row: photo, make and model, specification, year and mileage, and the highest offer at the bottom of the card, label left and amount right in large bold.
  • The card is the design system's product card, unchanged, used through its own slots; the buyer-side and business-seller listing cards are wrappers around it with their own logic, and this does not use them.
  • Make and model on one line (14px bold), the specification 12px and at most two lines, so every card in a row keeps one height. The price stays off the photo: these cards are much narrower than the ones dealers browse.
  • The photo is the front three-quarter shot, the first photo the funnel asks for, with the registration plate blurred before it is shown (server-side in production).
  • Cards run latest first, side by side in recency bands, each band's label above its first card and sticky while its cards scroll past. From 520px of width, arrows move the row two cards per press. 5 cards first; the last tile is "Näytä aiemmat".
  • Needs one more field per car: image, the front three-quarter photo with the plate blurred.
