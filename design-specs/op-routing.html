Design initiative · success page

OP routing

A pilot: while a seller waits for the review call or for offers, AutoVex suggests insurance for their next car from OP. One component, one button out to OP's insurance page, and no change to the selling flow.

Status: draft · 1 placement, 2 variants · copy and offer unconfirmed October 2026

What this pilot is

A seller selling their car will soon need insurance for the next one. We suggest OP's car insurance at a moment when the seller has nothing left to do in AutoVex, and send them to OP's own page to get a quote.

It must not cost the selling flow anything. The suggestion appears only where no action is asked of the seller, sits below everything AutoVex says on the page, and never replaces or delays a step.

One placement first. The success page after submitting, for sellers who have verified their email. Other placements follow once the concept is right.

Shared with Huutokaupat routing

Both initiatives are AutoVex suggesting a partner's service. The implementation differs (Huutokaupat routing is a full screen because the seller cannot continue with us; here they can), but the principles are the same:

  • AutoVex speaks. The suggestion is introduced as ours (“suosittelemme”, we recommend), in AutoVex's type and colours, and follows from what the page has just told the seller. It is a suggestion, not an advertisement: no frame, no banner.
  • The partner-branded button is the exit. The partner's mark and colour on the button, with an arrow, tell the seller they are leaving AutoVex. No separate “you are leaving” line. How loud the button is follows its role: Huutokaupat's is a filled, full-width button because routing is the only way forward there; OP's is outlined because it is a side suggestion.
  • Branding stays on the button. Nothing else on the block is the partner's.
  • It never deters the seller from what they still do with AutoVex. It appears only where nothing is asked of them, and below everything AutoVex says.

1 — Variant 1: OP insurance suggestion

Draft · draft copySuccess pageEmail verified: in review, or published

What changes

  • A short suggestion at the bottom of the left column, after everything AutoVex already says on the page. No frame, no rule, no image: text in the page's own style, so it reads as AutoVex speaking rather than as an ad.
  • The Hotjar rating survey stays last. In review, production already ends the column with Hotjar's “Kuinka helppoa asiointisi oli?” (how easy was it?) star rating; it stays below the block, untouched. The published screen has no survey.
  • Shown only when the seller has verified their email and the ad is either in review or published. Not shown while verification is pending (that is the seller's task), and not on a rejected ad or one missing photos.
  • It opens with AutoVex's own suggestion as body copy, in the page subtext's style (16px DM Sans): “Tutustu sillä aikaa suosittelemaamme tarjoukseen” (meanwhile, take a look at an offer we recommend). Both states it appears in end with the notice “Ota rennosti ja tarkkaile sähköpostiasi!” (relax and keep an eye on your email), so it continues that thought: there is nothing to do but wait, and this is something useful to do meanwhile.
  • All copy in the block is centred, like the page's own headline section.
  • The offer is the heading, in the same style and size as the page's headline (Barlow bold, 24px): “Uudelle autollesi vakuutus −50 %” (insurance for your new car, −50 %). One sentence, so the discount stays part of it. OP is named only on the button, which is where the seller meets OP.
  • The terms line sits under the button, small and grey, as the footnote to the click: “Koskee uusien kaskovakuutusten ensimmäistä vakuutusjaksoa.” (applies to the first insurance period of new comprehensive insurance).
  • The block keeps one shape without a border: the full-width button is exactly as wide as the notice card above, and the centred copy sits on the same axis as the page's headline.
  • One full-width outlined button, 48px tall: white, a 2px border in OP's orange, OP's round logo, “Tutustu OP:ssa” (explore at OP) and an orange arrow. The logo in the button is the only OP branding, and it is what says the click leaves AutoVex. It opens OP's car insurance page in a new tab.
  • Nothing else changes. The success page's content, the steps and the car card are untouched.

The grey lines are English glosses for reading, not copy. The heading follows on from the page's notice; the offer line speaks to the next car, not to the sale, since the seller already knows they are selling. The terms line keeps the offer line from promising more than the offer gives. Acceptance criteria

  • Control sellers see the success page exactly as today.
  • Variant sellers see the block only when their email is verified and the ad is in review or published, in every review-call timing.
  • The block never shows while email verification is pending, on a rejected ad, or when the publish was refused for missing photos.
  • The button opens OP's car insurance page in a new tab; the success page stays open.
  • Clicks on the button are recorded with the arm and the page state, so leads can be matched to what OP reports.

Why: right after submitting, a verified seller's task is done and they are thinking about changing cars, which makes this the most relevant moment with the least risk to the sale.

2 — Variant 2: OP perk behind a reveal

Draft · draft copySuccess page · modalEmail verified: in review, or published

What changes

  • The same offer, framed as a time-limited AutoVex perk rather than a suggestion: the seller should feel they would miss something, not that they are being advised. Same place and same states as variant 1.
  • On the page, one line and one button, in the pattern the decision page uses when a dealership has replied. The line: “Sinulla on 1 käyttämätön etu:” (you have 1 unused perk:) in Barlow bold 20px, centred. Under it, “Näytä AutoVex-edut” (show AutoVex perks), full width, the product's own secondary button (light blue, 56px) with a pulsing red “1” on its corner, as on the decision page's reply button. Nothing on the page names OP or the offer: the page asks only whether the seller is interested in a perk at all.
  • The button opens the coupon in a modal, the product's usual modal at 512px wide, with the product's usual modal header: “Käyttämättömät AutoVex-edut” (unused AutoVex perks). Plural on purpose: it suggests the perks vary over time and between sellers, which in this test they do not yet. Closes with the ×, Esc or a click outside; focus returns to the button.
  • The coupon: dashed edge; the expiry as a green tag in the top-right corner, “Voimassa 31.12.2026 asti” (valid until 31 Dec 2026) with an hourglass, saying the perk is still valid and will run out; OP's logo beside the heading “Uudelle autollesi vakuutus −50 %”; the code “AUTOVEX50” with the product's existing copy button.
  • The full terms, because the seller asked to see them: what the perk covers, for how long, the condition of moving insurances to OP, and where to enter the code.
  • The exit is the OP button: the product's own large button (56px, 16px label in regular weight, like every other button on the page) filled in OP's orange, with a white “Laske hinta OP:ssa” (calculate the price at OP) and a white arrow, opening OP's car insurance page in a new tab. No logo in it: the logo already sits beside the heading. Calculating a price commits the seller to nothing.
  • A partnership line closes the coupon, worded like Huutokaupat routing's: “AutoVex toimii yhteistyössä OP:n kanssa erillisinä palveluina.”

Acceptance criteria

  • Shown in the same states as variant 1, and never together with it.
  • The page shows only the reveal button; the coupon opens in a modal that closes with ×, Esc or a click outside, returning focus to the button.

Prototype, in review · prototype, published · design and copy above

When it shows

  • Success page only, desktop and mobile.
  • Shown when the seller's email is verified AND the ad is in review (every review-call timing) or published without review.
  • Not shown while the email is unverified (in review or queued for publishing), on a rejected ad (any reason), or when publishing was refused for missing photos. In those states the seller has something to do.
  • Not shown after the expiry date (end of 31.12.2026), whatever the arm.
  • Nowhere else: no other page, email or state.

On the page

  • Position: the bottom of the left column, after the notice card “Ota rennosti ja tarkkaile sähköpostiasi!” (relax and keep an eye on your email), 32px below it, as wide as that card. In review, production's Hotjar rating survey stays below it, last in the column.
  • Line: “Sinulla on 1 käyttämätön etu:”, Barlow bold 20px / 27.5px, black, centred. 16px below it, the button.
  • Button: the product's secondary large button, full width: blue-100 fill, blue-800 label, 16px regular, 56px tall. Label “Näytä AutoVex-edut”.
  • Count: a red “1” on the button's top-right corner, exactly as on the reply button of the decision page's offer card (24px red-500 circle, white 12px, pulsing, offset 4px out).
  • Clicking the button opens the modal. Nothing else on the page changes.

The modal

  • The product's standard modal (same overlay, panel, padding and × as the negotiation modal), with the panel capped at 512px instead of the default 896px.
  • Header: the negotiation modal's title row, “Käyttämättömät AutoVex-edut”, Barlow bold 20px / 27px, slate-900, with right padding so the × never covers it. It is the dialog's accessible name.
  • Closing: ×, Esc or a click on the overlay. Focus moves into the modal on open and back to the page button on close; the page does not scroll while it is open.
  • The coupon under the header: white, 2px dashed slate-300 border, 12px radius, 20px padding, 16px between parts:
    1. Expiry tag, alone in the top-right corner: the product's lime tag (lime-50 fill, lime-400 border, lime-700 12px medium) with an hourglass, “Voimassa 31.12.2026 asti”.
    2. OP's round logo (40px) beside the heading “Uudelle autollesi vakuutus −50 %”, Barlow bold 24px / 26px, slate-900. Non-breaking spaces keep “−50 %” together.
    3. Code row: “Etukoodi:” (14px slate-600), the code in a chip (16px bold, slightly spaced, slate-100 fill, slate-200 border), and the existing icon-only copy button from the seller contact card: copy icon, swaps to a check for 1.5 s after copying, accessible name “Kopioi koodi”, and “Kopioitu leikepöydälle” announced to screen readers. Fall back to a legacy copy where the clipboard API is refused.
    4. Terms: “Edun ehdot” (14px bold slate-900) and four bullet lines (14px slate-700).
    5. OP button: the product's large button (56px, 16px regular label, full width) filled in OP's orange #EE5A00, white “Laske hinta OP:ssa” and a white arrow, no logo. Opens OP's car-insurance page in a new tab (no opener); screen readers hear that it opens a new tab.
    6. Partnership line (12px slate-600).
  • Target URL: https://www.op.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/vakuutukset/ajoneuvovakuutus/autovakuutus-henkiloautolle until OP gives an attributed link.

Tracking

Each event carries the arm and the page state (in review or published). Names are suggestions; use the team's convention.

  • Eligible view: the success page shown in an eligible state, in both arms. The denominator for everything below.
  • Perk opened: click on “Näytä AutoVex-edut”. Answers “do sellers want a perk”.
  • Code copied: click on the copy button.
  • OP clicked: click on “Laske hinta OP:ssa”. Answers “does this offer convert”.
  • Funnel guard: the selling flow's own outcomes (review call, offers accepted) compared by arm, so a perk that distracts would show.

Acceptance criteria

  • Control: the success page is unchanged in every state.
  • Variant: the line and button appear only in the eligible states, never with variant 1, and not after the expiry date.
  • The modal opens from the button and closes with ×, Esc and an overlay click, returning focus to the button.
  • The header fits beside the × at 360px wide; the coupon has no horizontal scroll at 360px.
  • The copy button puts the exact code on the clipboard, shows the check for 1.5 s and announces it.
  • The OP button opens the target in a new tab; the success page stays open behind it.
  • All four events fire once per action with the arm and page state.

Do not

  • Name OP, the offer or the discount on the page itself; they belong in the modal.
  • Open the modal automatically or remember that it was opened.
  • Put OP's logo in the button, or change the page's other content.
  • Pass any seller or car data to OP in the link.

Blocking before release

  • OP: the code (does their purchase flow take one, and is it AUTOVEX50), the expiry date, the terms wording, and an attributed link or parameter for lead counting.
  • Legal: whether the partnership line is enough disclosure, and what the link may carry.
  • Contrast: white 16px on #EE5A00 is 3.4:1, below AA for normal text. Agree a darker orange with OP or accept it knowingly.
  • Test setup: confirm the arms (assumed: control against variant 2; whether variant 1 runs as well is open) and the split.

Later placements

Not built. The component is meant to move to these with its context adapted:

  • Email during the auction. The same proposition as a text block and the branded button, in the email that tells the seller their ad is published and the auction can begin.
  • Offers page and decision page, while the auction is still running and the seller is waiting.
  • Profile, as a permanent spot after the pilot.

Open questions

With OP

  • Which offer can we communicate, and how? The “−50 %” offer and its terms line are drafts until OP confirms the wording and conditions.
  • Are campaign codes supported, for example an AutoVex-specific code in OP's purchase flow?
  • How are leads attributed to AutoVex, and what counts as a qualified lead? This decides what the button's link carries.
  • The logo and the button's colour need OP's approval; they are taken from OP's public site for this draft.
  • Contrast of the orange button in variant 2. White 16px text on OP's orange is 3.4:1, below the 4.5:1 accessibility minimum for normal text (OP's own site uses the same pairing). Options: a darker orange agreed with OP, or a larger label.
  • The target page. OP's car insurance page for now; a direct link to the calculator, or a landing page of OP's, may convert better.

With Legal

  • What, if anything, the link may carry to OP. Today it carries nothing about the seller. Any identifier or car data passed for attribution needs a legal basis and may need consent.
  • Does the suggestion need a marketing label? It is designed to read as AutoVex's own suggestion, not an ad. Consumer law requires marketing to be recognisable as marketing and to show on whose behalf it is made; whether OP's name and logo on the button are enough, or a label such as “Mainos” or “Yhteistyössä OP” is needed, is for Legal.
