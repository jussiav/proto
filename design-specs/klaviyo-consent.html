Design initiative · profile page

Klaviyo consent

Klaviyo becomes the only store of email marketing consent, and a new email address on the profile page takes effect only once the seller confirms it. This page is design's proposal for what the seller sees in both, with every status message and copy string in one place. It was sent to the team on 11 November 2026, and the copy is final.

Status: proposal sent to the team · ready to build once the team agrees · copy approved November 2026

Summary

What changes for the seller

  • Marketing emails setting (section 1): the checkbox leaves the contact details form and becomes a switch in a section of its own. It shows what Klaviyo says and saves straight to Klaviyo the moment it is switched, with its own messages for loading, saved, failed and Klaviyo not answering.
  • New email address (section 2): “Päivitä” no longer changes the address at once. A link goes to the new address, the field keeps the address in use, and a red line under it says which address is waiting.
  • Confirmation email (section 3): the developers' wording, with one change to the body. Approved.

See it in the prototype · see production today

What stays as it is

  • The rest of the profile page: name, phone, terms, “Päivitä” and the password section.
  • The ad's contact step: ticking its marketing box still subscribes the email in Klaviyo.
  • An address already used by another account is still refused with today's error.

Trying it in the prototype

The prototype bar's Events menu sets the seller's state in Klaviyo (subscribed, unsubscribed from an email, never subscribed), makes Klaviyo stop or start answering, and opens the confirmation link. An inbox notice in the corner stands in for the email.

1 — Marketing emails setting

Ready to build · copy approvedProfile pageLogged-in seller opens the page

What changes

  • Its own section, “Markkinointiviestit” (marketing emails), between the contact details and the password section. Same heading style and dividers as the password section; the label is today's checkbox label.
  • A switch, not a checkbox. A switch takes effect at once, while a checkbox reads as something a save button confirms, and this section has none. It is the switch dealers already use in the negotiation form (for car pickup), exactly as it looks there, without the outlined row around it and without that row's icon. There is no switch component of its own in the product to reuse. Clicking the label or the switch switches it, and Enter or Space on the switch.
  • The switch's own colours: AutoVex Blue (#0B6DFF) when on, slate-400 (#94A3B8) when off, slate-300 (#CBD5E1) while it saves, a white knob. The label is black, with nothing drawn around the label and the switch.
  • Two small additions, needed for a label this long: a 16px gap between the label and the switch, and a switch that never shrinks. Without them the label squeezes the switch from 48px to 38px. The dealer's short label never shows this.
  • Saved on its own, at once. Switching saves straight to Klaviyo; “Päivitä” no longer carries the setting. The switch moves immediately and shows its disabled look while it saves, so it can't be switched again; a failed save moves it back.
  • Shows what Klaviyo says. The page asks Klaviyo when it opens. The switch is on only when the seller is subscribed; unsubscribed and never subscribed both show it off.
  • If Klaviyo doesn't answer, the switch is hidden and one line says the setting couldn't be fetched. A switch shown off would wrongly tell the seller they are not subscribed.
  • Single opt-in is assumed. With double opt-in, switching on only sends a confirmation email, and the section would need a “waiting for confirmation” state (open question 3).

Acceptance criteria

  • On opening, the section shows “Haetaan asetusta…” until Klaviyo answers, then the switch: on if subscribed, off otherwise.
  • If Klaviyo doesn't answer, the switch stays hidden and the unavailable line shows instead.
  • Switching on subscribes and switching off unsubscribes the seller in Klaviyo, at once, with no save button. Clicking anywhere on the row, or Enter or Space on it, switches it.
  • The switch can't be switched again while it saves. A successful save shows “Asetus tallennettu.”; a failed one moves the switch back and shows the failed message.
  • The switch keeps its full 48 × 24px size next to the long label, at every width.
  • The switch looks exactly like the dealer's car-pickup switch, with no outline or background around the label and the switch.
  • “Päivitä” saves the contact details without the marketing setting.
  • An unsubscribe made from a Klaviyo email shows as off the next time the page opens.

2 — New email address applies once confirmed

Ready to build · copy approvedProfile pageSeller saves a new address

What changes

  • Today “Päivitä” changes the address at once, with no message and no email.
  • After saving a new address, the name and phone save as before. The email field goes back to the address in use, a toast says where the link went, and the confirmation email (section 3) goes to the new address.
  • A red line under the field names the address waiting and asks the seller to check their email. It uses the same style as the error messages under the password fields (red-500, 14px, 6px below the field). It stays until the address is confirmed, also after a reload.
  • Saving another address replaces the waiting one and sends a new link.
  • Opening the link changes the address and shows “Sähköpostiosoitteesi on vaihdettu.” The address on the seller's ads changes with it, as it does today, and the Klaviyo profile and its consent move to the new address.

See it in the prototype — change the email, press “Päivitä”, then open the link from the inbox notice.

Acceptance criteria

  • Saving a new address keeps the current one in use, sends the link to the new one, and shows the toast with the new address.
  • The red line under the field shows the waiting address until it is confirmed, also after a reload.
  • Saving a different new address replaces the waiting one; only the latest link works.
  • Opening the link changes the address, removes the red line and shows the changed toast.
  • Saving without changing the address sends nothing and leaves a waiting address as it is.
  • An address another account uses is refused with today's error, before any link is sent.

Open questions

  1. Preference page link. Can we link to a seller's own Klaviyo preference page from outside Klaviyo's emails? Klaviyo's documentation says the link only exists inside its own emails. If not, the switch in section 1 is the way.
  2. Reading the setting. Can the profile page ask Klaviyo for the seller's subscription when it opens? Section 1 needs it to show the switch on or off.
  3. Single or double opt-in. With double opt-in, switching on only sends a confirmation email, and section 1 needs a “waiting for confirmation” state.
  4. The link. How long is it valid, what does an expired or already used link show, and does the old address get a notice? These decide the last missing texts.
  5. Transactional emails. To confirm: unsubscribing from marketing doesn't stop transactional emails, such as new offers and negotiation updates.
  6. The complete-profile page has the same marketing checkbox. Does it change the same way, and who reaches that page today? It has a save button, so the checkbox could stay there.
