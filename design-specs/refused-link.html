Refused link page

When a link in one of our emails no longer works, the seller lands today on an empty ad form with no explanation. This page replaces that with three versions, the same three as the current build: log in to continue, ask for a new confirmation email, and fill in a new ad. Every refused link lands on one of them. Each says roughly why, and above all how to continue.

Status: draft for discussion · copy is a draft November 2026

Summary

How the page works

  • The version depends on the ad, not on why the link failed. An ad with an account: log in. An ad without one that can still be continued: a new confirmation email. An ad that is gone: a new ad. That covers every refused link with three versions; which version, when lists the situations.
  • An orange notice says roughly why, in two short lines: the link has expired, or the ad has expired. It doesn't explain the link change itself: from the seller's side, an old link is simply expired.
  • The heading, text and button say how to continue. Where the ad still exists, the text starts with “Myynti-ilmoituksesi on tallessa.”, so the seller knows nothing was lost.
  • Every version ends with production's support sentence, linking the support page.
  • Built on the page that stops a seller whose car has too much mileage (Huutokaupat routing), with the ads page's orange notification instead of its red one: this is something to act on, not a stop.

Trying it in the prototype

Open the page. The prototype bar's Scenario menu has each version and its states. Events shows or hides the plate and mileage, and switches the send button on this page on or off.

“Lähetä vahvistusviesti uudelleen” works: pressing it shows the sent state and, after a moment, the confirmation email in the corner, whose button continues to the waiting screen as confirmed. The fourth press shows the limit message.

Which version, when

Checked in this order; the first that applies decides. “Has an account” means the ad is tied to a seller account that still exists: someone confirmed the ad's email address, or the seller was logged in when they started the ad.

The versions

Each preview is the prototype page at phone width, with the plate and mileage shown. Every version has the same parts: the logo (to the front page), the orange notice, a heading and text, the action and the support sentence.

1 · Log in to continue

When: the ad has an account. Action: the login page, which sends a login code to the account's address. For the profile's email-change link the text instead starts “Sähköpostiosoitteesi ei vaihtunut.” and asks to change the address again on the profile.

Open · email-change link

2 · Ask for a new confirmation email

When: no account, the ad can still be continued. Action, as proposed: the waiting screen's own “Lähetä vahvistusviesti uudelleen”, here on the page. The text says the email goes out when the button is pressed, and to which address: the one on the ad, masked as Jaakko proposed (the first two letters of the name and of the domain, ma****@ex****), since anyone holding the link sees the page. The same masked address is in the text after sending. See open question 1.

Open · without a send button

3 · Fill in a new ad

When: the ad is gone. Action: the front page's form; an expired ad doesn't block a new ad for the same car. “Takaisin etusivulle” is left out, since the button already goes there. The plate and mileage are there only when the ad expired, not when it was deleted or not found.

Open

2 · after sending

The heading says it went, the text says what to do with it. The button becomes a text link, so sending again stays possible without being the main thing on the page.

Open

2 · too many sends

Three per ad in ten minutes, ten per address in an hour. The login page's own rate-limit message, in its error box, above the button.

Open

2 · without a send button

If the page can't send, the seller asks for the email from the ad itself. That only works on the device they filled the ad in on, since the way back to an unconfirmed ad is stored in that browser; the text has to say so.

Open

Plate and mileage: optional

Why they help: seeing their own car's plate and mileage tells the seller at a glance that the ad is still stored and not lost. The page reads fine without them.

Whether they fit is Jaakko's call. What we know from the code: the server loads the ad from the link before it checks the link, so plate and mileage are at hand whenever the ad still exists and the link isn't damaged. Today's refusal is a plain redirect that passes nothing on, so they would have to be handed to the page in the session, never in the address: a registration number in a URL ends up in logs and analytics. Whether to show an ad's details to someone holding a refused link is part of that call.

What has to change with it

  1. The duplicate-ad screen. When a seller starts a second ad for a car that is already in review or waiting for confirmation, the screen says “Pääset jatkamaan aiempaa myynti-ilmoitustasi linkistä, jonka lähetimme sähköpostiisi.” (You can continue your earlier ad from the link we sent to your email.) For a seller whose link is refused, that screen and this page send them to each other. The duplicate-ad screen needs the same “send a new confirmation email” action, or text that points to it.
  2. Send limits before a send button goes live. Anyone who has an ad's id can ask for a confirmation email for it, and the resend has no limit of its own today. The limits this page shows (three per ad in ten minutes, ten per address in an hour) are in a change that is still open.
  3. A deleted ad. A link to a deleted ad ends on a “not found” page before the link is checked at all. To reach version 3, that case has to come here too.
  4. The email-change link. In the change being built for it, a refused link ends on an error page; it should come here as version 1.
  5. Customer service. Briefed before the release on the three versions, and that the support page is the way on for an ad whose account was deleted.

Open questions

  1. Send button on the page (Jaakko). Is sending a new confirmation email from this page planned or feasible? Design proposes it, because the alternative, asking for it from the ad, only works on the device the ad was filled in on. The copy differs between the two (see version 2 and “without a send button”); the version with the button must not suggest an email was already sent.
  2. Plate and mileage (Jaakko). Can the page show them? See plate and mileage.
  3. “Has an account”. Is the line between versions 1 and 2 in the current build the one described in which version, when?
  4. Already logged in. Open the ad directly (row 0) instead of showing the page?
  5. After logging in, the seller lands on their ads page, not on the ad the email was about. Should the login return them to it?
  6. Deleted accounts. The address of a deleted account can't confirm a new ad until that night's anonymisation clears it. Intended? Until it is decided, version 3's support sentence is the way on.
  7. Email change. Fine with the Klaviyo consent work that its refused link lands on version 1?
