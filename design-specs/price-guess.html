Design initiative · hinta-arvio page

Price guess — variant 5

The hinta-arvio page as an email sign-up. A visitor types the plate and the mileage, gives an email address and marketing consent, and gets an AutoVex-arvio for the car on the page and by email. No login, no tracker, no contact details. The other variants of the initiative are collapsed at the end of this page.

Status: in design · copy is a draft · open questions below November 2026

Summary

What variant 5 is

  • A landing page for car owners looking for a price estimate, not yet ready to sell.
  • The estimate for an email address: the email step asks for an address and marketing consent, then shows the estimate and sends it by email.
  • The estimate card, AutoVex-arvio, is the page’s focus and the same in the welcome email.
  • A way into the full service for a visitor ready to sell: “Aloita kilpailutus” with the car, email and consent already filled in.
  • No estimate without a car: if the lookup finds no car, the flow stops and no email is asked for.

See it in the prototype

What it is not

  • No login, no email verification and no contact details step.
  • No value tracking: no history, no forecast, no monthly estimate on the page.
  • Not the sales funnel: the email flow after the welcome email is Marketing’s.

Trying it in the prototype

The prototype bar’s Scenario menu has every screen. Events switches the marketing consent between required and optional and brings the welcome email back. Seed car → Car details makes the lookup find nothing or only part of the car.

1 — Landing page

Where · hinta-arvio pageScenario · Landing

What it shows

  • The hero as v4 has it, with the subtitle “Katso autosi hinta-arvio rekisterinumerolla ja ajokilometreillä.” and the plate and mileage fields. 280 000 km and over gets the front page’s error, as everywhere else.
  • Sections in this order: “Näin auton hinta-arvio lasketaan” (no button), “Mikä vaikuttaa autosi arvoon?”, the FAQ, then “Valmis myymään autosi?” for a seller ready to sell now, with the “Miksi AutoVex?” list, the Google rating and “Aloita kilpailutus” to the front page, as the nav button.
  • Nothing about tracking the value: no “Seuraa”, no monthly estimate, no history.
  • What the estimate is based on is said openly: “AutoVexin markkinadataan”, led by offers dealerships make and sellers accept, plus model, age and mileage, and “Kehitämme arviota jatkuvasti”. It names no single data source, so the way the estimate is made can change without changing the copy. The same wording is in the FAQ, the estimate card, the welcome email and the page’s search description.
  • The FAQ and the page’s description for search engines follow the v5 answers.

See it in the prototype

Who the page is for

A car owner who arrives from search looking for a price estimate and is not yet ready to put the car up for auction. The page gives the estimate in exchange for an email address. The last section is for the visitor who is ready to sell now.

The front page shows no section for this variant.

2 — Email and marketing consent

Where · hinta-arvio page, funnel layoutScenario · Email and marketing consent

What it shows

  • The ad creation funnel’s layout: logo, three steps (Auto · Sähköposti · Hinta-arvio) and the cream column. No site navigation or footer.
  • “Löysimme autosi tiedot!”, then “Anna sähköpostisi, niin lähetämme sinulle hinta-arvion autostasi.”. The copy promises the estimate by email, not on the page: told they will see it on screen, a visitor may type a made-up address, and we need the real one.
  • The success page’s new-email illustration, not the funnel’s “car found” picture, since this flow asks nothing more about the car.
  • The email field, then the funnel contact step’s marketing checkbox. Required by default: Legal’s current reading is that the estimate cannot be given without marketing consent (to be confirmed). Unticked: “Hyväksy sähköpostiviestit jatkaaksesi.”.
  • “Lähetä arvio” leads straight to the estimate page. No login, no verification link, no contact details step.
  • The cream column: the car as the car card shows it, without a photo (a car found by its plate never has one) and without buttons, the mileage beside the plate; then the demand card.

See it in the prototype

Trying the optional consent

The prototype bar’s Events → Marketing consent switches the checkbox between required and optional. With it optional and unticked, the estimate page names no email address and the phone shows an empty inbox: what that visitor receives is an open question.

3 — Car not found

Where · hinta-arvio pageScenario · Car not found

What it shows

  • Shown after the loading screen when the lookup finds no make and model. No email is asked for and there is no way forward: no estimate can be promised for a car we could not find.
  • “Emme löytäneet autoasi” and “Emme löytäneet autoa näillä tiedoilla, joten emme voi tehdä sille hinta-arviota. Tarkista rekisterinumero ja yritä uudelleen.”
  • “Hakemasi tiedot”: the plate as the reg plate badge and the mileage, as the visitor typed them.
  • “Yritä uudelleen” returns to the landing hero with both fields filled and the plate selected, ready to correct.
  • One centred column: no steps and no cream column.
  • If the lookup finds the make and model but not everything, the visitor still gets an estimate, and the car shows only what was found.

Reaching it from the hero

Prototype bar: Seed car → Car details → Not found, then submit the hero. Partial gives the estimate with part of the details.

4 — Price estimate

Where · hinta-arvio page, funnel layoutScenario · Price estimate

What it shows

  • The funnel’s layout, not the logged-in pages’ light blue. Only the estimate is large; everything else is kept light.
  • The estimate card, AutoVex’s deliverable to the visitor, styled like the decision page’s offer card (blue border, blue ribbon), without any seal icon:
    • Ribbon “AutoVex-arvio”, the estimate’s own name, with the date it was made opposite it.
    • The two things the visitor typed side by side: the reg plate badge and the mileage, in the plate badge’s box.
    • The estimate, Barlow bold 56 px.
    • What the lookup found, as the car card shows it: make and model, specification, then year, drive and fuel.
    • The grey footer: “Arvio muuttuu ajan ja ajokilometrien myötä.” and what the estimate is based on.
  • “Valmis myymään? Anna autoliikkeiden kilpailla autostasi.” with the page’s only “Aloita kilpailutus”, into the funnel with the car, the email and the consent already filled in, then “Näin löydämme autollesi ostajan”, the front page’s three steps.
  • The cream column: “Mikä vaikuttaa autosi arvoon?” with both lists in one card.
  • The three section titles are the same size: 24/30.

5 — Welcome email

Where · the visitor’s inboxScenario · Price estimate + welcome email

What it shows

  • Sent once the email step is done. In the prototype a phone appears beside the estimate page after 1.4 s; Events → Inbox brings it back.
  • Subject “Tervetuloa AutoVexiin! Tässä autosi hinta-arvio”, then “Tervetuloa AutoVexiin!” and “Tässä autosi hinta-arvio:”.
  • The same estimate card as the page, a size smaller.
  • “Kun haluat myydä”, the body under it and “Aloita kilpailutus” into the funnel.
  • “Lähetämme sinulle jatkossa mielenkiintoista sisältöä ja vinkkejä auton myyntiin.”, then “Et halua enää viestejämme? Peru tilaus”.

See it in the prototype

A proposal for Marketing

There is no welcome email in Klaviyo today that could be used without welcoming the visitor into the sales funnel, so this is design’s proposal. Marketing writes and builds the real one and the flows after it, which later move subscribers to the regular marketing emails.

Behaviour and data

  • The estimate in the prototype is a stand-in figure moved by the mileage. Which tool makes the real one is open, and the copy is written so that it can change.
  • The date on the estimate card is the day the estimate was made, kept with it. A new plate or mileage makes a new estimate and a new date.
  • What the visitor typed (plate and mileage) sits together beside the plate; what the lookup found (make, model, specification, year, drive, fuel) is shown as the car card shows it.
  • The lookup: no make and model means “Car not found”. Make and model found means an estimate, with whatever else was found.
  • Marketing consent is required by default (Legal’s current reading, to be confirmed). Optional is a prototype setting for discussing the open question.
  • Into the funnel: “Aloita kilpailutus” opens the ad creation funnel at the equipment step with the car already found, and the email and consent in the contact step.
  • The 280 000 km limit and the plate format are the front page’s.

Open questions

  1. Marketing consent and the estimate (Legal). Can the estimate be given without marketing consent? The current reading says no; to be confirmed.
  2. Without consent (Marketing, Legal). If consent becomes optional, which email, if any, does a visitor who leaves the box unticked receive?
  3. The car and the regular flow (product). Does the car enter the regular sales flow, as a draft, once the email is given?
  4. The estimate (product, Analytics). Which tool makes the estimate, and what it is based on.
  5. Several cars per email (product). How a visitor who values more than one car is handled. Variant 5 offers no “Arvioi toinen auto” button until this is settled.
  6. “AutoVex-arvio” (Marketing). The estimate’s own name, beside the word “hinta-arvio” the rest of the page uses. Marketing to confirm before it goes beyond the prototype.
  7. The welcome email and its flow (Marketing). Marketing writes and builds them; the email here is a proposal.
