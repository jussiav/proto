Design initiative · hinta-arvio page

Price guess — variant 6

The hinta-arvio page with an AutoVex account. A visitor types the plate and the mileage, fills in the ad creation funnel’s contact step and verifies their email, then sees a free AutoVex-arvio for the car on a logged-in page and in an email. Marketing consent is optional. The earlier variants of the initiative, variant 5 among them, are collapsed at the end of this page.

Status: in design · copy is a draft · open questions below November 2026

Summary

What variant 6 is

  • A landing page for car owners looking for a price estimate, not yet ready to sell.
  • The estimate after an account: the visitor fills in the ad creation funnel’s contact step (name, phone, email, terms, optional marketing consent) and verifies their email. The verification link logs them in and opens the estimate.
  • The estimate page is a logged-in page, on the light blue the offers and decision pages use, with the estimate card at an offer card’s width.
  • The estimate email is a service email to everyone who verified. Only those who ticked marketing consent are added to the marketing emails.
  • A free, authentic AutoVex-arvio: the copy says the estimate is free and where the genuine one comes from.
  • The front page remembers the car: a seller who comes back sees the car and its AutoVex-arvio, and continues into the ad creation funnel from there.

See it in the prototype

Why it changed from variant 5

Legal: marketing consent cannot be a condition of the service, and the estimate by email counts as a marketing email unless the visitor is logged in or has consented. Variant 5 required consent to see the estimate, so every visitor now gets an account instead, and consent is optional.

What it is not

  • No value tracking: no history, no forecast, no monthly estimate.
  • Not an ad: verifying creates a user, not a draft. The car enters the funnel only when the seller presses “Aloita kilpailutus”.

Trying it in the prototype

The prototype bar’s Scenario menu has every screen. Events → Inbox brings the estimate email back. Seed car → Car details makes the lookup find nothing or only part of the car.

1 — Landing page

Where · hinta-arvio pageScenario · Landing

What it shows

  • Hero: “Maksuton hinta-arvio autostasi”, with the subtitle “Katso autosi AutoVex-arvio rekisterinumerolla ja ajokilometreillä.”, the plate and mileage fields and the Google rating. 280 000 km and over gets the front page’s error, as everywhere else.
  • Sections in this order: “Näin auton hinta-arvio lasketaan” (no button), “Mikä vaikuttaa autosi arvoon?”, the FAQ, then “Valmis myymään autosi?” for a seller ready to sell now.
  • What the estimate is based on is said openly: “AutoVexin markkinadataan”, led by offers dealerships make and sellers accept, and so reflecting the demand for similar cars right now. It names no single data source, so the way the estimate is made can change without changing the copy.
  • Free and authentic: one word for free, “maksuton”, throughout. The how section adds “AutoVex-arvio on maksuton. AutoVexissä on myyty jo yli 80 000 autoa, ja kehitämme arviota jatkuvasti, jotta se olisi mahdollisimman tarkka.” The FAQ asks whether the AutoVex-arvio costs anything and how to recognise the genuine one.
  • Six FAQ items. The page title, the search description and the FAQ markup for search engines follow them.

See it in the prototype

Who the page is for

A car owner who arrives from search looking for a price estimate and is not yet ready to put the car up for auction. The last section is for the visitor who is ready to sell now.

The front page shows no section for this variant; it shows the seller’s car once they have an estimate (section 7).

2 — Contact details

Where · hinta-arvio page, funnel layoutScenario · Contact details

What it shows

  • The ad creation funnel’s layout: logo, three steps (Auto · Yhteystiedot · Hinta-arvio) and the cream column with the car (no photo, the mileage beside the plate) and the demand card. No site navigation or footer.
  • “Löysimme autosi tiedot!”, then “Täytä yhteystietosi, niin näet autosi hinta-arvion.”
  • The funnel’s contact step, as it is: full name, phone number, email, the terms (required) and the marketing checkbox (optional), with the funnel’s own error messages.
  • “Jatka”, with the note “AutoVex-arvio on maksuton. Lähetämme sähköpostiisi vahvistuslinkin, josta pääset suoraan arvioon.”
  • A visitor who is already logged in skips this step and goes straight to the estimate.

See it in the prototype

Why the whole contact step

Every visitor who wants the estimate becomes a user of the platform, so the step asks what creating a user needs, exactly as the ad creation funnel does. The estimate is shown once the email address is verified.

4 — Car not found

Where · hinta-arvio pageScenario · Car not found

What it shows

  • Shown after the loading screen when the lookup finds no make and model. No contact details are asked for: no estimate can be promised for a car we could not find.
  • “Emme löytäneet autoasi” and “Emme löytäneet autoa näillä tiedoilla, joten emme voi tehdä sille hinta-arviota. Tarkista rekisterinumero ja yritä uudelleen.”
  • “Hakemasi tiedot”: the plate as the reg plate badge and the mileage, as the visitor typed them.
  • “Yritä uudelleen” returns to the landing hero with both fields filled and the plate selected.
  • If the lookup finds the make and model but not everything, the visitor still gets an estimate, and the car shows only what was found.

5 — Price estimate

Where · hinta-arvio page, logged inScenario · Price estimate (logged in)

What it shows

  • The logged-in pages’ look, as the offers and decision pages: light blue behind a 768 px column, section titles in blue, white cards, the account menu in the navigation (no marketing menu) and the app’s short footer.
  • The top is the decision page with one offer: its hero on the left (the round no-photo car, “Autosi hinta-arvio on valmis!” and “AutoVex-arvio on maksuton, eikä se sido sinua mihinkään.”) and the estimate card at an offer card’s width (336 px) on the right. On a phone the hero comes first and the card takes the full width.
  • The estimate card: ribbon “AutoVex-arvio” with the date opposite it; the plate and mileage together; the estimate; the make, model, specification, year, drive and fuel as the car card shows them; the grey footer with what the estimate is based on.
  • “Valmis myymään? Anna autoliikkeiden kilpailla autostasi.” in two columns from 768 px: the text on the left, “Aloita kilpailutus” and “Autosi tiedot siirtyvät mukana.” on the right. Stacked on a phone.
  • “Näin löydämme autollesi ostajan” (the front page’s three steps), then “Mikä vaikuttaa autosi arvoon?” with both lists.
  • “Usein kysytyt kysymykset”, the offers page’s accordion, with four questions: what the AutoVex-arvio costs, how to recognise the genuine one, what it is based on, and whether you have to sell. Then “Muuta kysyttävää? Vieraile tukisivulla”.
  • Section titles have no icons yet.

7 — Front page, coming back

Where · front page heroScenario · none: open the estimate, then the front page

What it shows

  • A logged-in seller who has an AutoVex-arvio and no ad yet sees that car in the front page hero instead of the plate and mileage fields.
  • The hero’s existing card for a car in progress: “Laitetaan autosi liikkeelle:”, the plate, make and model, and the mileage, plus a row with the “AutoVex-arvio”, its date and the estimate.
  • “Aloita kilpailutus” opens the ad creation funnel at the equipment step with the car already found and the seller logged in, so the contact step later asks for nothing new.
  • “Tee uusi tarjouspyyntö” hides the car from the hero on that device and shows the fields again. Nothing is deleted: an estimate is not an ad.
  • Once the seller has started the ad, the hero shows the draft as it does for anyone, with “Jatka” back to the step they left, and keeps the estimate row until the ad is sent.

Behaviour and data

  • The account: the contact step’s details create the user when the email is verified, as the ad creation funnel’s verification link does. Verifying creates no draft.
  • Marketing consent is optional. The estimate email goes to everyone who verified; only those who ticked the box are added to the marketing emails.
  • Already logged in: the contact step and the verification are skipped, and the estimate opens straight after the loading screen.
  • The estimate in the prototype is a stand-in figure moved by the mileage. Which tool makes the real one is open, and the copy is written so that it can change.
  • The date on the estimate card is the day the estimate was made, kept with it.
  • What the visitor typed (plate and mileage) sits together; what the lookup found (make, model, specification, year, drive, fuel) is shown as the car card shows it.
  • The lookup: no make and model means “Car not found”. Make and model found means an estimate, with whatever else was found.
  • Into the funnel: “Aloita kilpailutus”, on the estimate page, in the email or on the front page, opens the ad creation funnel at the equipment step with the car already found.
  • The front page shows the car and its estimate to a logged-in seller with no ad (section 7).
  • The 280 000 km limit and the plate format are the front page’s.

Open questions

  1. “Aina maksuton” (management, Legal). The FAQ says the AutoVex-arvio is always free. That is a promise to customers and needs sign-off before it goes beyond the prototype.
  2. The sender address (developers, Marketing). The FAQ names tiimi@autovex.fi as the address the estimate comes from; the estimate email has to be sent from it.
  3. The phone number (product). The contact step asks for it, as the funnel does. The FAQ does not yet say why an estimate needs it.
  4. An account with only an estimate (product). The account menu leads to the offers page, which has nothing to show such a seller. Whether the estimate is kept with the account, and where a seller finds it again, is open; today the front page hero and the email carry it.
  5. The estimate (product, Analytics). Which tool makes it, and what it is based on.
  6. Several cars per account (product). How a seller who values more than one car is handled. The hero shows one car.
  7. “AutoVex-arvio” (Marketing). The estimate’s own name, beside the word “hinta-arvio” the rest of the page uses. Marketing to confirm before it goes beyond the prototype.
  8. The marketing emails after consent (Marketing). What a seller who ticked the box receives, and when.
