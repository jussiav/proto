Enhanced success page

A seller who skips the photo step still fills in everything else and presses submit. What they get is a red-framed error telling them the bidding cannot start — and nothing telling them the rest of their work was saved. This initiative proposes the opposite order: confirm what we received, then say what is missing, then show what is left to happen.

Status: proposal · 1 change · copy is a draft September 2026

How to read this

Nothing here is agreed yet. This is a proposal for the team, in the same shape as the other spec pages: one change, stated as what the seller sees before and after, with the prototype carrying both so you can flip between them.

The Finnish copy is a draft. Production has no wording for what is proposed, so every string in the After column is written for this page and needs a copy round before it could ship. The Before column is production, verbatim.

It is one change today. The initiative is named for the surface rather than the fix so that further work on the end of the funnel can join it, the way Enhanced negotiations grew in batches.

First: in production this is not a success page

The screen is the publish step refusing to publish. Submitting with fewer than five photos does not create a listing. The draft stays open — its card on the same screen reads Keskeneräinen (“unfinished”) — and the seller is shown an error and sent back.

That is why the screen has no illustration and no “what happens next” list, where every other ending of the funnel has both: there is no next yet. Technically correct, and it is the whole problem.

The seller does not know any of that. They answered every question, uploaded nothing, pressed the last button, and landed on a red frame. From where they sit this is the end of the funnel — so it is the end of the funnel that has to answer them.

Exactly what the cream column does here — three separate gates

Worth spelling out, because the column is easy to get wrong in either direction: it looks empty beside the other endings, but it is not empty — and the three things that could be in it are decided by three unrelated conditions. Two of them disagree on this state.

In the columnHere?Because

Do not confuse it with the other missing-photos screen

There are two in production, with different triggers, different copy and different framing. This initiative is only about the first.

TriggerHeadline todayIn scope?
Seller skips photos and submits“Lisää vielä kuvat autostasi”Yes — this page
Listing was reviewed and bounced back for photos asked for on the call“Huomio! Tarvitsemme sinulta lisätietoja.”No — left exactly as it is

The second one already does what this proposal asks for: it acknowledges, states what is needed in its own card, and shows the remaining steps. It is a reasonable model for the first — and it has been checked against production line by line, so it can be trusted as one.

One thing on it is a production quirk, and it should be left alone: its step list still tells the seller the listing is being reviewed, even though the review already happened and sent it back. That falls out of how the step list is computed, and it is faithful rather than a slip. If you end up on that screen for other reasons, do not quietly correct it — raise it.

What changes

  • The order of what the seller is told. Receipt first, then the gap, then what remains. Today it is only the gap.
  • The red frame goes. Nothing has failed — the seller has one task left. An error frame at the end of a completed funnel reads as work lost.
  • An illustration and the “Mitä tapahtuu seuraavaksi?” list arrive, matching every other ending of the funnel. Three steps: add photos → verify email → listing is checked.
  • The button names the task, not the object — “Lisää puuttuvat kuvat” rather than “Lisää kuvat”.
  • Unchanged: the five-photo minimum, the destination of the button, the car card and its Keskeneräinen badge. The draft is still not published, and nothing here pretends otherwise.

See the proposal in the prototype · see production — both links go straight to the state. Seed a car from the prototype bar first if the page has no vehicle. Walking the funnel and skipping the photo step reaches the same screen.

Acceptance criteria, if it goes ahead

  • The seller is told their details were saved before being told what is missing.
  • Nothing on the screen reads as a failure: no red frame, no error styling.
  • The five-photo minimum is still stated, and the button still goes to the photo step.
  • The cream column and the car card in it survive, and the card still shows the draft as unfinished. The listing is not published and the screen must not imply it is. This is the easiest thing to lose while removing the error framing.
  • The Finnish has been through a copy round. Nothing on this page has.

Why: The seller has just answered every question in the funnel. Told only that the bidding cannot start, in a red frame, the reasonable reading is that the submission failed and the work is gone — so the ones who do come back come back anxious, and the ones who do not are lost at the last step of a funnel they had finished.

Open questions

  • Is dropping the “why photos help” sentence right? It is the only line that explains the requirement rather than stating it. It could stay as a quieter line under the button.
  • Does the three-step list belong here at all? It describes a path that only starts once photos are added. It may read as premature — or as exactly the reassurance the screen lacks.
  • Step 3's wording depends on Review / No review, as above.
  • Is there a measurement? Worth knowing how many sellers reach this screen and how many return to add photos, before and after. Nobody has looked.
  • Does anything else at the end of the funnel belong in this initiative? The other endings were checked against production and match it; this is the only invented screen. Further work would join here rather than starting a new page.
