Design initiative · ad creation funnel

Review / No review

The funnel tells every seller the same story about what happens after they submit — including the call from our team. For sellers outside the review segment that call never comes. This initiative makes funnel communication match the process the seller is actually in, one change at a time.

Status: live — 1 change ready to build Arms: control · v1 Prototype param: ?review-no-review= August 2026

Why this initiative exists

Production already knows, at the price step, whether a seller's car is inside the review segment. It just doesn't tell the seller a matching story.

What production computes

One boolean, resolved server-side and handed to the frontend on the draft resource:

TenderRequestReviewSettings (Filament: enabled, fuel_types,
  min/max_model_year, min/max_mileage)
  → EvaluateDraftForReview
  → resource: can_review

The settings are a whitelist — ReviewIfPreferredSegment checks the car falls inside the configured band, so a high-mileage or old car is not review-called. PriceInfo.vue is the only component that reads can_review: it swaps the optional price estimate for a required asking price, and submitting publishes the draft instead of sending it to review.

What the seller is told

WhatHappensNext.vue takes three props — draftStatus, isVerified, missingImages. There is no can_review anywhere in it. At the price step the draft status is still open, so every seller gets the full five-step list, bullet 2 of which is:

—

—

A seller whose car is outside the segment reads that promise on the same screen where we ask them for an asking price because they are outside it. Their ad publishes on submit; nobody calls. The promise is broken by the next click.

The list already varies — just on the wrong input

The five bullets drop to four when the seller is logged in (isVerified removes verify your email). So the component does branch — on session state, never on whether the seller's car gets reviewed. The one branch that omits the review bullet is queued_for_publishing_after_verification, which is exactly the status a non-reviewable seller reaches after submitting. The correct information exists one screen too late.

Change log

A sibling initiative ships first. Seller intent adds one question per arm to the same price step, testing how ready the seller is to sell. It deliberately leaves this initiative's two changes alone — the "Mitä tapahtuu seuraavaksi?" panel stays and the submit copy stays — so it does not depend on work that lands later. Once intent can influence the outcome, Change 1 stops being a fidelity fix and becomes necessary: no screen at or before the price step can honestly say what happens next.

The initiative spans several funnel steps. Each change is specced, built and reviewed on its own; new ones are appended to this table and get their own section below. One arm parameter covers all of them, so a walkthrough is always in one consistent world.

Production implementation — exhaustive

  1. One line in Sidebar.vue. Drop || props.formState.matches('providingPriceInfo') from shouldShowWhatHappensNext. Nothing else in that computed changes, and WhatHappensNext.vue itself is not touched — it still serves the review, verification, publish-queue and success states.
  2. Decide the mobile case deliberately. shouldShowSidebarOnMobile is shouldShowWhatHappensNext || providingPersonalInfo, so removing the price step from the first also stops the sidebar rendering on mobile at that step. That is a consequence, not a decision — confirm it is what you want before shipping. On mobile the step then shows the price question alone.
  3. Do not fill the gap. VehicleMetrics is already in that column (v-if="vehicleMetrics?.brand") at every step, so on desktop production keeps showing it after the panel goes. No new component, no new copy.
  4. Leave can_review alone. This change adds no new consumer of it. PriceInfo.vue stays the only one.

Prototype implementation

  1. The panel is one unit. Heading and bullets sit in #price-what-happens-next; the arm toggles that wrapper. The wrapper repeats the column's own gap-6, so nesting it changed no spacing in control.
  2. The column stays on desktop, goes on mobile. Same flex-1 max-w-[48%] cream panel at full height, holding only the (dormant) save-draft mount. Below 768 px it is hidden instead: stacked under the form it would be a bare 64 px strip of cream padding, and production hides the sidebar there anyway for the same reason (point 2 opposite).
  3. The empty desktop column is a prototype artefact. VehicleMetrics was never ported to this step, so the panel reads blank here while production keeps showing that card. Do not treat the blank column as the specified design.
  4. Control keeps all of its logic. The login-state bullet drop and the badge renumbering are untouched, and the nextSteps.* strings stay in translations.js. In the v1 arm that work is skipped rather than run against a hidden panel.
  5. Orthogonal to the page's scenarios. ?scenario=price-estimate|asking-price still chooses the price question; the arm chooses the communication. All four combinations are reachable.

Already correct in production — do not change

Things the prototype used to get wrong about a no-review seller, all verified against the current code. They are listed here because they look like initiative material and are not: production already behaves correctly, and the prototype's control arm was corrected to match. Nothing to build.

The call is not promised after the price step

PublishDraft marks a draft for review only when EvaluateDraftForReview passes. Otherwise an unverified seller is queued (queued_for_publishing_after_verification) and a verified one publishes outright — and both of those statuses take WhatHappensNext branches that drop the review step ([allNextSteps[0], ...slice(2)] and slice(3) respectively). So the email-confirmation screen never promises the call to a no-review seller. The price step is the only screen that does, which is exactly the scope of Change 1.

The car card is not shown on every screen

shouldShowPreview is isWaitingForReviewInReview || canPreviewDraft, and canPreviewDraft does not include success or a rejected draft in waitingForReview — while Success.vue draws no card of its own. So the card appears on the in-review and email-verification screens and nowhere else: a rejected or published draft shows none. That is also why Preview.vue's draftStatusConfig.published — the untranslated literal 'Published' with a warning-octagon icon — never renders, and should be read as dead config rather than as a badge anyone sees.

The estimate field carries no label

In PriceInfo.vue the reviewable seller's estimate is a bare NumberInput with a placeholder and no label prop — only the asking-price field passes one (asking_price_label, marked required). The prototype used to draw a label above both. Corrected in the control arm: the estimate variant now shows the question in the placeholder (Oma arvio (vapaaehtoinen)) and its info line reads Ei vaikuta tarjouskilpailuun eikä velvoita myymään., both from production; the asking-price variant keeps its label and star. The label difference between the two variants is production's, not this initiative's.

The seller's own price is never echoed back

Preview.vue passes asking_price into CarCard, and CarCard never renders it — the prop is declared and unused. CarDetailsCard, the modal behind the card's own CTA, has no price field either. (PreviewModal.vue does render one, but no component imports it.) So neither the contact step nor the confirmation screen shows the asking price or the estimate back to the seller.

How this was verified

All four walked from the price step through the contact step to the confirmation screen, with no ?scenario= forcing anything — the prototype derives the state from the same two facts production does (was the car in the review segment, has the email been confirmed). The verification email's link is followed for the two verified rows. The pairing worth stating: a no-call seller who has not yet verified lands on the queued screen, which mentions no call at all, and the page badge and the card badge agree.

Change 2 — the contact step stops naming an outcome

The last click before submitting reads "Lähetä tarkastukseen" — personal_info.submit in PersonalInfo.vue, with no can_review branch. It is already wrong for a seller outside the review segment, and once the seller-intent answer can downgrade a seller it is wrong for some inside it too. The arm replaces it with one neutral copy.

One copy, not three

An earlier draft varied the button by outcome — send for review / publish / submit. That was dropped deliberately: three copies would leak the decision one step before we are ready to state it, and the seller would learn their outcome from a button. One neutral copy promises nothing and denies nothing, which is also why nothing on this step branches on the outcome at all.

Implementation notes

  • No outcome branching. The arm decides the string; the seller's segment and intent answer are not consulted on this step.
  • The arm owns the element, not data-i18n. The attribute was removed and the label is applied by the page, so a language switch cannot overwrite the arm's copy — the same pattern the price step uses for its own variant strings.
  • The offers-return case is untouched. When the step is opened from the offers flow the button is a plain "Valmis" and stays that way in both arms.

Candidates — under discussion, not specced

Listed so the initiative's shape is visible, not as work to pick up. Nothing here is decided, and none of it is in the prototype yet.

  • Where the process story belongs instead. If the price step no longer carries it, the seller still needs to know what happens after submitting — and by the success step the draft status makes the answer accurate for free.
  • The bullet list keyed to login state. Today the five/four split is driven by isVerified; the review bullet itself never varies. Whether the list should branch on the segment wherever it does appear is an open question.
  • The submitted-state copy. review_comms_content exists only for drafts in review, so a non-reviewable seller's post-submit screen is already a different path — worth reading end to end before adding anything new.
  • Dead config worth deleting. Preview.vue's draftStatusConfig.published entry cannot render (see above). Cleanup, not a seller-facing change — listed so nobody rediscovers it and mistakes it for a live bug.

Initiative lifecycle

How this page and the prototype stay honest as the initiative moves. The same rules apply to every initiative — they are recorded in the prototype's CLAUDE.md.

  1. Live. The status chip in this page's header says ready to build or in test; the arms are switchable in the prototype from the initiative's own bar row.
  2. Promoted. When an arm becomes the default, the losing arms are deleted along with the initiative's entry in window.protoPage — the bar row disappears and the parameter stops being read. Copy that nothing renders any more goes too: if v1 wins here, the whole nextSteps namespace leaves translations.js, since the price step is its only consumer.
  3. Completed. This page stays, with the header chip changed to completed — <arm> promoted, <month year> and a line naming what shipped. In CLAUDE.md the initiative moves to the completed list as one line. The record survives; the switch does not.
