Design initiative · details step

Informed ad creation

Sellers tell us they would have liked to know what the ad asks for before they started, the photos above all. A collapsed help panel on the first step, the same one the photos step uses for its photo tips, lets careful sellers prepare, without slowing down everyone who just starts.

Status: first draft · 1 variant · copy is a draft October 2026

Why

Sellers ask for it in the funnel feedback. They would have liked to know before starting which photos the ad needs. Today they find out on the photos step, the third of five, often away from the car.

The service book is the other thing worth having at hand. Sellers do not mention it, but for a car with a paper service book, photos of the service entries are one of the strongest additions to a listing.

Likely a front-end A/B test first. It adds one panel to one step and stores nothing, so it can run as a front-end test in VWO to measure whether it is worth building properly.

It must not become a gate. Most sellers should simply start, so the checklist is collapsed behind one link, and its first sentence says they can start right away.

What changes

  • A link directly under “Tarkista ja täydennä tarkemmat tiedot…”, built exactly like “Vinkkimme parhaisiin kuviin” on the photos step: the same expandable help component, with the info icon instead of the camera.
  • Collapsed by default, every visit. Closed, it costs one line. Nothing else on the step moves.
  • Opening it folds out the same grey help panel as the photo tips: a heading, a close × in the corner, the content, and a “Sulje” button at the bottom. Tapping the link again, the × or “Sulje” closes it.
  • The heading says they can start right away (“Voit aloittaa heti”), so the panel reads as optional help rather than a requirement.
  • Two items: photos (at least 5, which ones) and service details, which include photographing the entries of a paper service book.
  • Its copy is its own. The photo tips are about photos only; this panel covers the whole ad, so the two share the component and not the wording.
  • Nothing is stored or checked. The panel does not change any later step.

The grey lines are English glosses for reading, not copy. The wording is new, but every fact in it is what the funnel already asks or already tells the seller: the 5-photo minimum, the draft being saved, and the last-service and repairs questions.

Acceptance criteria

  • Control sellers see the details step exactly as today.
  • Variant sellers see the link under the subtitle, collapsed, on every visit.
  • It uses the same expandable help component as the photo tips, with the info icon and its own copy.
  • Closing with the × or “Sulje” returns focus to the link.
  • Opening and closing does not change any field, validation or the Jatka button.
  • Opening it is recorded with the arm, so we can read how many sellers open it.

Why: sellers ask to know what the ad needs before they start; telling them on the first step, only if they ask, helps the careful ones without slowing down the rest.

What to read from it

  • Open rate: how many sellers on the details step open the checklist.
  • Did it cost anything? Details-step completion, variant against control. A list of things to gather could make some sellers leave to come back later, and some never do.
  • Did it help? Among sellers who opened it: photo count, the share reaching the 5-photo minimum, the share skipping photos, and service-book photos on cars with a paper book.
  • Did the request go away? Mentions of “I'd have liked to know beforehand” in the funnel feedback, before and after.

Open questions

  • Is the details step early enough? A seller who wants to prepare has already typed their plate and mileage by now. The same block could sit on the front page, under the hero form, for sellers who are not ready to start.
  • The icon. The component's own default trigger icon does not exist in the icon set, so this uses the plain info icon. Worth confirming with whoever owns the component.
  • Electric cars: a battery health report is worth having at hand too, and it fits once documents can be attached (see Seller file upload).
