Success colour: lime to green

The success colour on the current button component and on the tab component moves from lime to the brand green. This page has the decisions, the exact classes, and every state drawn with its contrast measured.

For the dev building Enhanced negotiations change 15. Decided by UX, 9 October 2026. Read against the production code of 7 October 2026.

Decisions

  • Q12
    Repaint all five button variants — default, primary, secondary, ghost and link — in AButton.vue. Each uses brand green steps and passes WCAG 2.0 AA in every state. Classes in section 2.
  • Q12
    The primary label is black. Confirmed. The label is 14–16px, so it needs 4.5:1. White and the dark green steps both fail on a green bright enough to read as the main action; black on green-400 is 12:1.
  • Q11
    Repaint the tab too. UiTab.vue's success colours move to the same green steps as the button. Classes and states in section 3.
  • Scope
    UiButton.vue is not changed. The dealership's three accept buttons run on it and stay lime until that team moves them to AButton. That was agreed with them on 17 September.
  • Seller
    All three seller accept buttons end up green. Two already use AButton and change with the colour. The offer card's accept moves from UiButton to AButton. See section 1.

  • primary: label black in every state. green-900 drops to 4.0:1 on hover.
  • secondary: label green-900. green-700 fails on all three green fills.
  • default, ghost, link: the pressed label stays green-700. The lighter green-600 fails on white and on green-50.

Every state below is drawn from the classes above at the md size (14px label) and measured. Today's lime is shown for comparison; seven of its states fail. All fourteen green states pass.
